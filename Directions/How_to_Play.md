# How to Play The Midnight Market Exchange

## Goal

Help the six story vendors find the neighbor who can make a fair trade with them. Discover every vendor clue, complete all three trading pairs, and then gather at the fountain to finish the evening.

## Start the Game

1. Open `project.godot` in Godot 4.
2. Press **Play Project** (or **F5**).

## Controls

- **W, A, S, D** - Move around the market.
- **E** - Talk, choose a vendor, introduce two vendors, open the Town Hall puzzle, or interact with the fountain when prompted.
- **Mouse** - Select squares and number buttons in the mini-Sudoku window.

Watch the prompt near the bottom of the screen to see what pressing **E** will do nearby.

## Trading Puzzle

1. Walk close to a story vendor and press **E** to ask about their wares and needs.
2. Read the clue added to the **Vendor Notes** panel.
3. After learning a vendor's clue, press **E** near them again to choose them for an introduction.
4. Find another known vendor whose wares meet the first vendor's need and who needs what the first vendor sells.
5. Press **E** near that vendor to introduce the pair.

A correct pair will meet, trade, and return to their stalls. A wrong guess does not cause a penalty, and the first vendor stays selected so you can try someone else. To cancel your selection, return to the selected vendor and press **E**.

The task panel tracks both objectives:

- Learn all **6 vendor clues**.
- Find all **3 trading pairs**.

## Final Task

After learning all six clues and completing all three trades, the market will invite you to the central fountain. Walk close to it and press **E** to complete the game.

## Optional Mini-Sudoku

Walk to the Town Hall puzzle door and press **E** to open a 6-by-6 mini-Sudoku.

- Click an empty square, then click a number from **1-6**.
- Each row, column, and 2-by-3 box must contain every number from 1 through 6 once.
- Use **Erase** to clear the selected square.
- Use **New puzzle** to generate a different puzzle.
- Click the **X** in the puzzle window to return to the market.

The mini-Sudoku is optional and is not required to complete the trading story.

## Tips

- Ask every story vendor for a clue before making introductions; unfamiliar vendors cannot be selected as partners.
- Match both sides of a trade: each vendor must sell what the other one needs.
- Completed vendors cannot be paired again.
- Ambient shoppers and stallkeepers can be visited, but only the six vendors listed in **Vendor Notes** are part of the main puzzle.
